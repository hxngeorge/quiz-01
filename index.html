HOW TO PLAY >>

THE RULES

Three new questions, the same for everyone, every day. Answer all three to finish today's run — one attempt per day.

POINTS

Each question shows its own points before you answer. Some questions have several parts worth points separately — get one part right and you still earn its share, even if you miss the rest.

LEADERBOARD

TODAY ranks everyone who's played today, ties broken by whoever finished fastest. ALL TIME adds up every day you've played.
