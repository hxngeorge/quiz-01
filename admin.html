i HOW THE GOOGLE SHEET WORKS

One row per day. Columns, per question (Q1, Q2 and Q3 each have their own):

  • date — YYYY-MM-DD. A row only reaches players when this is today's date.
  • status — draft, live or archived. Only live reaches players; open index.html?preview=true to rehearse a draft yourself.
  • qN_text, qN_type (text or mcq), qN_points, qN_answers, qN_opt_a..d (multiple choice only), qN_image (optional).
  • Multiple choice: put the right answer's letter (A–D) or its exact text in qN_answers.
  • A typed question with several parts (e.g. "name both X and Y"): qN_points like 2+2, qN_answers like mercury | venus — one number per part, separated by |.
  • Pictures: a file path (images/q1.png), a Google Drive share link, or any https:// address.

Templates with these columns already filled in: sample-sheet.xlsx (with dropdowns and filters) · sample-sheet.csv (plain text)

This page only reads your settings — it can't change them. To change the Sheet ID, the Firebase project, or the password, edit firebase-config.js directly.
